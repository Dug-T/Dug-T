You are WallyMan, trapped in a neon maze. Collect every spark to clear the level. Grab a flare and the Sentries panic, so hunt them for bonus points. Bonus fruit appears twice a level, so grab it before it fades. Double-tap the screen (or press F) to breathe fire at the Sentries. Fire burns fuel, so pick up fuel cans to top up. Every level is a new maze, and from level 3 the Titan boss joins the hunt: flares don't scare it, only fire takes it down.
