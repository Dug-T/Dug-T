WallyMan Maze

You are WallyMan, trapped in a neon maze. Collect every spark to clear the level. Grab a flare and the Sentries panic, so hunt them for bonus points. Bonus fruit appears twice a level, so grab it before it fades. Double-tap the screen (or press F) to breathe fire at the Sentries. Fire burns fuel, so pick up fuel cans to top up.
