How to survive

The volcano will erupt before the clock in the corner runs out. Find a way off the island, or a way to live through it:

⛵ Raft: 10 driftwood or palm logs, 3 ropes (braid vines) and the tarp from the plane wreck. Build it at Raft Beach and paddle past the reef.
🚤 Skiff: Kai's boat lies by the village jetty. Patch it with 2 logs and a rope and fit the plane's battery, then motor out past the reef. The battery is also what the radio needs, so choose.
📡 Radio: find the battery (wreck), antenna (ranger tower) and handset (village radio hut). Repair the ranger radio and board the helicopter at the helipad.
🚪 Bunker: stock the old bunker with 4 waters, 4 meals and an ash mask, then seal the door and wait it out.

Food: grab crabs on the beaches, and craft a fishing spear (a log, a stone and a vine) to spear fish from the shallows. Raw food makes you ill, so cook it: tap USE at a lit fire. Goats live on the high ridges. Build a lean-to (3 logs, 2 vines, 3 palm fronds) for shelter from the ash and a place to sleep. The ranger's quad bike at the station is quicker and climbs steeper than the jeep, and seats two. Rain squalls fill your canteen if you stand out in them. Twelve pages of Dr Osei's journal are scattered over the island. Photo mode is in the pause menu.

The lighthouse on the western cliffs (🗼) has a lamp you can light with 2 logs and some cloth; once it's lit, the helicopter finds the island much faster. From the unrest onwards, sulphur gas pools in the hollows below the cone: if you smell rotten eggs, get uphill. Listen to the observatory's emergency broadcasts in the jeep or at the ranger station. Tap the map to set a waypoint.

Some survivors won't leave without something they've lost. Ask them to come with you and they'll tell you what; it's then marked on your compass and map. Old Pete won't go anywhere without Biscuit, his dog.

Night falls once during the game, during the ashfall. Build a campfire (2 logs, 2 stones) and hold USE beside it to rest and recover, or tap USE to sleep till dawn (you can also sleep under a roof). Sleeping restores you fully, but the eruption carries on while you sleep; craft a torch (a log and some cloth) and it lights itself in the dark and in thick ash.

Keep eating, drinking and resting. When the ash falls, wear a mask or get under a roof. Bring the other survivors with you: talk to them, then press again and they'll follow. The survey jeep by the camp seats you and three passengers; it won't swim and it won't climb cliffs, and lava and falling rocks will wreck it.
