Objectives

    FIELD MANUAL

    • Build: pick a structure in the sidebar. When it says READY, click it again and place it near your base.
    • Economy: Harvesters gather glowing crystal and bring it to your Refinery. Blue crystal is worth double.
    • Power: keep the ⚡ bar green. Low power slows production and switches off Plasma Towers.
    • Select: left-click, or drag a box. Shift adds. Double-click selects all of that type on screen.
    • Command: right-click ground to move, right-click enemies to attack. A + click = attack-move, S = stop.
    • Groups: Ctrl+1-9 to assign, 1-9 to recall (tap twice to jump to them).
    • Camera: arrow keys, screen edges or right-drag to scroll, mouse wheel to zoom, Q/E to rotate, H home, Space jump to last alert.
    • Buildings: select a factory and right-click to set a rally point. R repair mode, X sell mode.
    • Engineers capture enemy buildings. Wally (hero) snipes infantry and plants C4 on buildings.
    • Veterancy: units with 3 kills become Veterans (★), 6 kills Elite (★★) and self-repair.
    • Factions: the Wally Squad has Titan tanks, Field Medics, Wally Man (Gatling gun), Wally and the Sky Lance. The Grimlock Legion has Shade stealth tanks, Inferno flame tanks and the Doom Missile.
    • Unit Designer (main menu): invent your own infantry and vehicles. They appear in the Barracks and War Factory build menus.
    • Support powers (buttons above the build tabs): Recon Sweep, Paradrop and Airstrike recharge over time. Click one, then click the map.
    • Research (TECH tab): one-off upgrades for armour, damage, income, speed and range.
    • Stances (selection panel): Aggressive, Hold position or Hold fire.
    • Game over when all your buildings are destroyed. Destroy every enemy building to win.
    • Sandbox (main menu): spawn anything, anywhere. The Sky Hauler flies 6 infantry over any terrain.
    • Hovercraft cross water and lava. EMP Strike (Tech Lab) disables enemy machines for 12 s. Units with 10 kills become Heroic.
    • Fighting back: units under fire attack the shooter automatically and nearby idle friends help (unless set to Hold fire or given a move order).
    • Find enemy base: 🎯 button or N - points you at the enemy base, then jumps between enemy buildings you've found.
    • Patrol: press P (or the Patrol button) and pick a point - units walk back and forth, fighting on the way.
    • Warlord Grak (Grimlock hero): G = War Cry, nearby Grimlocks fire much faster.
    • Idle units: the 💤 button (or .) jumps to fighting units with nothing to do.
    • Repair Truck fixes vehicles in the field; the Recon Drone scouts and reveals cloaked tanks.
    • Rotate the map: twist two fingers, tap ⟲ / ⟳ in the top bar, or tap the compass to reset (Q / E on a keyboard).
    • Medevac Copter heals units beneath it; Supply Drop power parachutes in crates; beware the Blood Moon - every weapon hits harder!
    • Hero Hunt (skirmish option): protect your crowned champion and kill theirs. Blink Trooper teleports where you send him; the Tunnel Drill can't be hit while it's moving underground.
    • Bagpiper (Wally Squad) / War Drummer (Grimlocks): nearby allies fire 25% faster. Keep them behind your army!
    • Shield Projector (Wally Squad): units inside its bubble take 40% less damage. Bone Catapult (Grimlocks): long-range boulders that flatten buildings.
    • Spy: enemies ignore him. Right-click an enemy building to sneak in - steal cash from a Refinery, black out a Power Plant or reset their superweapon. Attack Dogs can sniff spies out!
    • Wally Mech (Wally Squad) and Cave Troll (Grimlocks): giant late-game units. The Troll heals itself, so finish it off!
    • Bounty Convoy: when the armoured cash truck appears, destroy it before it escapes for a $1,500 bounty.
    • Reinforcements support power: two tanks parachute onto the spot you pick.
    • King of the Hill (skirmish option): hold the glowing ring in the middle of the map with no enemies inside. First to 3 minutes wins.
    • Wyvern Rider (Grimlocks) breathes fire from the sky; the Tesla Truck (Wally Squad) is a mobile Tesla Coil.
    • Grenadier (Wally Squad) lobs grenades over walls; the Goblin Boomer (Grimlocks) runs at the enemy and explodes. Shoot Boomers before they reach you!
    • Tesla Coil (Wally Squad) and Flame Tower (Grimlocks): faction defences. Lightning arcs between up to 3 enemies; the Flame Tower roasts anything close.
    • Civilian houses: right-click a neutral house with infantry to move up to 3 inside. They shoot from the windows and the house is yours while they stay.
    • Battle Bunker (Defence tab): right-click it with infantry to garrison up to 4. They shoot out safely; U evacuates.
    • Daily Challenge & Mutators: a new challenge every day, plus optional rule changes in Skirmish.
    • APC: select infantry and right-click the Squad Carrier APC to load up to 5. U unloads them.
    • New to RTS? Play Boot Camp from the main menu for a step-by-step tutorial.
    • Map Editor (main menu): build your own maps. Photo mode: F9.
    • Survival (main menu): hold out against endless waves for a high score.
    • Waypoints: hold Shift and right-click to queue up a route.
    • Wally Man's Gatling Frenzy: select Wally Man and press G (or the button) for 8 seconds of super-fast fire and speed.
    • Supply crates drop on the battlefield: drive over them for money, repairs, promotions, reinforcements... or a trap!
    • Tech buildings: grey neutral buildings (Crystal Derrick, Field Hospital, Repair Depot) can be captured with an Engineer.
    • Superweapons: build the Uplink / Silo after a Tech Lab. When charged, press LAUNCH in the sidebar and click a target.
    • Walls, gates & mines (Defence tab): drag to lay a wall line. Gates open for your units only. Mines are invisible to the enemy.
    • Weather & night: rain and snow slow vehicles, fog, blizzards, sandstorms and darkness cut sight range.
    • Touch: tap to select / command, one finger drag to scroll, pinch to zoom, use Box select for drag-selecting.
